== 2026-09-09 | the installed HTTPRoute CRD, read back from the cluster ==
The entry's claim that the retry stanza exists at these pins rests on this file:
the CRD the cluster actually serves, not on the manifest that installed it.

$ kubectl get crd httproutes.gateway.networking.k8s.io -o jsonpath='{.metadata.annotations}'
