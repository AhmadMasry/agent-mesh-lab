WHAT DIFFERS FROM THE PLAN, OR NEEDS A WORD
  - The brief asked for a pad in "a field the receiver ignores". a2a-go ignored the top-level x_pad member; a2a-python
    did not: it answered -32600 "Invalid request. Extra fields: {'x_pad'}". The Python receiver's pre-dispatch ledger
    recorded the arrival (method SubscribeToTask, body_len 2200000), and its SDK dispatched nothing.
  - The batch probes carry no identity in their bodies, and the receivers' ledgers fill identity only from a JSON object,
    so make ledgers found no line by work-item id and exited 2 (its recipe's "no ledger lines of any kind"). Their
    arrival and response lines were found by the body hash the client recorded, and kept as ingress-by-body-hash.txt in
    each batch directory; counts.py reads them from there. For every refused send make ledgers also exits 2 and writes
    no ledger file; the refusal and the absence of ledgers are what counts.py reads.
  - A SubscribeToTask dispatched by the SDK is recorded in the execution ledger as received and result, with no
    execute line. counts.py counts received lines (sdk_received) beside executes.
  - curl's padded sends went with Expect: 100-continue, and their response.txt holds the interim 100 Continue block
    before the final status; counts.py skips it.
  - The clean check's own curl pod (gate2-curl) was still Terminating at 19:38:33Z, which is that script's cleanup. This
    step's curl pod (c6-curl) was deleted at 19:37:34Z.
