## the Jaeger chart's Service publishes more ports than the manifest's
# The manifest publishes the three the lab uses (4317, 4318, 16686). The chart
# publishes fourteen: every listener the all-in-one image has, including the Jaeger
# agent's UDP ports, Zipkin, the v1 collector ports, the gRPC query port and the two
# internal-metrics ports. A Service port creates no listener -- the listeners are the
# image's either way -- so this changes nothing the lab measures, and the three the
# collector's exporter and `make export-trace` address are among them. Recorded as a
# difference, not corrected: trimming the Service would mean overriding a chart
# template rather than a value.
