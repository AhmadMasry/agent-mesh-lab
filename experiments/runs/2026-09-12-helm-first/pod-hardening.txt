## what the charts allowed and what they did not
Allowed, and set:
  read-only root filesystem, allowPrivilegeEscalation false and all capabilities
  dropped on all three, through `securityContext` (collector, jaeger) and
  `server.containerSecurityContext` (prometheus). All three came up with it; the
  host-side `docker run --read-only` check taken before the rebuild had already shown
  the images tolerate it (readonly-rootfs-check.txt).

  no ServiceAccount token on the collector (`serviceAccount.automountServiceAccountToken:
  false`) and on the trace backend (`jaeger.serviceAccount.automountServiceAccountToken:
  false`).

Non-root, and at which level it is set -- corrected 2026-09-12 (fix round 1), because this
  paragraph first said every chart sets it at the pod level, which is not true of jaeger:
  collector    runAsNonRoot and runAsUser 10001 at the POD level, set by
               otel-collector-values.yaml (podSecurityContext)
  jaeger       runAsNonRoot at the CONTAINER level, set by jaeger-values.yaml
               (jaeger.securityContext); runAsUser 10001 at the POD level, the chart's own
               default (jaeger.podSecurityContext)
  prometheus   runAsNonRoot and runAsUser 65534 at the POD level, the chart's own default
               (server.securityContext)
  Rebuild #2's rebuild-2/pod-hardening.txt prints each field at both levels.

Not allowed by the chart:
  Prometheus keeps its ServiceAccount token. It has to: the five scrape jobs use
  `role: pod` and `role: endpoints` Kubernetes discovery, which reads the API with that
  token. `server.automountServiceAccountToken: false` exists in the chart and was not
  used, for that reason.

  The Prometheus chart's ClusterRole is wider than the manifest's. The manifest grants
  get/list/watch on nodes, services, endpoints and pods -- "the smallest role that
  role: pod and role: endpoints need", in its own words. The chart's adds
  nodes/metrics, ingresses and configmaps, plus ingresses and ingresses/status in
  networking.k8s.io. The chart offers no narrower one: only
  `server.useExistingClusterRoleName`, which would require the role to come from a
  manifest and so would put half of this component back on the other route. Recorded as
  a difference; it does not stop the Helm route reproducing the lab.

  The Jaeger chart's Service publishes fourteen ports where the manifest publishes
  three. A Service port opens no listener -- the listeners are the image's on both
  routes -- so nothing measured moves, and trimming it would mean overriding a template
  rather than a value.
