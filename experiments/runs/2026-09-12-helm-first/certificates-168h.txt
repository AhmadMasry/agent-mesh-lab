## the arithmetic, stated exactly rather than rounded
# The brief asked for NOT AFTER minus NOT BEFORE equal to 168h on every leaf. The
# measured span is 168h02m, and the two minutes are not noise to be waved past:

   agentgateway-ingress                                                  
      NOT BEFORE 2026-09-12T18:24:28Z   NOT AFTER 2026-09-19T18:26:28Z
      NOT AFTER - NOT BEFORE = 7 days, 0:02:00 = 168.0333 h
      168h would be 2026-09-19T18:24:28Z
   default                                                               
      NOT BEFORE 2026-09-12T18:24:28Z   NOT AFTER 2026-09-19T18:26:28Z
      NOT AFTER - NOT BEFORE = 7 days, 0:02:00 = 168.0333 h
      168h would be 2026-09-19T18:24:28Z

# Read the other way, the requested lifetime is exact. Both leaves were issued at
# 18:26:28Z -- the moment the new ztunnel pods came up, 18:26:27Z and 18:26:28Z --
# and 2026-09-12T18:26:28Z + 168h is 2026-09-19T18:26:28Z to the second, which is
# the NOT AFTER both carry. NOT BEFORE is two minutes EARLIER than issuance, i.e.
# backdated. So the issued validity is exactly the requested 168h forward from
# issuance, plus a constant two-minute backdating of NOT BEFORE.

# That the two minutes are a constant and not a property of the new lifetime is
# evidenced by the reading taken before the change, in certificates.txt: the 24 h
# leaf read NOT BEFORE 2026-09-12T17:52:34Z, NOT AFTER 2026-09-13T17:54:34Z, which
# is 24h02m by the same subtraction and 24h exactly forward from its 17:54:34Z
# issuance. The offset is therefore unchanged by this task, and no document was
# consulted for its mechanism, so none is claimed here -- it is recorded as
# observed on both readings.
