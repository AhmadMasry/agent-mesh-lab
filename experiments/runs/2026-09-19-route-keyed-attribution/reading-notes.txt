WHERE A RECORD READS OTHER THAN A READER WOULD TAKE IT:

 a. fixture-rows/rows.txt shows "row R3:go exit status 1 / stopping", then R3:go again sixteen seconds later with
    exit status 0. The first is not a failed row. The row driver ran in the background and its twelve rows were
    going to take longer than ten minutes; whether the harness that ran it ends a background command at that mark
    could not be checked, and a row killed mid-way would have skipped its restore. So the driver was stopped
    between rows by the matrix script's own guard: a placeholder fixture-rows/r3-go/summary.csv holding one row
    of another nonce, which the script refuses BEFORE anything is sent or changed (its message is the line
    above the status). The placeholder directory held that one file and was deleted; the driver was started
    again for R3:go and R3:py. No row was run twice and no repetition was discarded. (The first driver ran
    10 min 20 s, so the harness does not end a background command at ten minutes.) rows.log itself is not
    committed (`*.log` under experiments/runs/ is ignored); rows.txt is its row lines, by grep.
 b. rows.sh is the driver with one line changed (it named the checkout by an absolute path); the line says so.
    host-sleep.sh is the previous task's recorder with its two header lines changed; its first line says so.
 c. In every Python-receiver row the matrix script unsets DOWNSTREAM_A2A_URL for the row and puts it back, and in
    R3 it sets the receiver's model-retry knob and puts it back: two rollouts per such row. The worker pod and the
    orchestrator pod of cluster-after.txt are therefore not the pods the previous task's records name, and
    those work items' ledger lines can no longer be collected from a live pod; the Deployments' knob values
    are the ones the first row of this task found (probe/r2-waypoint-go/knobs.txt, "-- before --").
 d. "no injection armed on the mock" is read from the mock's own log, which writes one line per inject and per
    reset and whose pod did not restart: its last control line is a reset, after its last inject. The two
    receivers write no such line. For them: the orchestrator pod started at 13:18:32Z, after its last arming at
    13:17:59Z (a new process holds no arming); the worker pod started at 13:10:29Z and was armed three times
    after that (13:14:07Z retry-mechanics waypoint, 13:15:15Z retry-mechanics ingress, 13:16:51Z proof row R2
    waypoint go, and nothing else: every arming is a line of a control.txt here), each of those three work items
    shows its two arrivals, which is the arming firing once and disarming itself, and every run ended with
    POST /control/reset -> 204 on all three (each run's control.txt, and "injector resets on this exit path" in
    each matrix row's knobs.txt).
 e. The `http_status` column is empty on every proxy row, as it always was: agentgateway writes `http.status`,
    and the exporter reads `http.response.status_code` / `http.status_code`. Not changed by this task; the
    proxy's status for a span is in that work item's trace.json.
 f. retry-mechanics took about 30 s between "pre-run" and "stanza on" for ingress and egress and 1 s for
    waypoint: the script waits for the previous run's control pod to finish terminating before it starts its own.
