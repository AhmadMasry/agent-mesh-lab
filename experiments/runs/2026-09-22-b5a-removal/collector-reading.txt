== what this reads, and what it does not
   Each of the two pods was still reaching the collector shortly before its own SIGTERM -- the last record naming
   the first is stamped 2026-09-22T19:27:00.344Z, 1.400 s before its SIGTERM, and the last naming the second is
   stamped 2026-09-22T19:44:27.974Z, 3.477 s before its SIGTERM -- and neither reached it again afterwards. So the five seconds
   are the span processor's shutdown timing out, which is the pod's own line, and not the collector being
   unreachable. This lab exports at 100 % sampling to a collector in another namespace, so those five seconds
   are a property of this configuration rather than of the proxy.
   What this does NOT read: why the processor's shutdown timed out, and anything about the other 30 removals,
   whose drains began 0.291-0.587 ms after SIGTERM and which carry no such line.
