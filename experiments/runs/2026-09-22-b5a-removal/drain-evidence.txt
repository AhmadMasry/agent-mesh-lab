WHAT WAS MEASURED, IN ONE SENTENCE
  With one HTTP response streaming through an agentgateway proxy, the proxy is asked to shut down; the proxy announces
  that it will wait up to 55 s for open connections to complete and then ends the stream 10.0 s after its minimum
  drain expires (agw-central, the HBONE listener, with the WARN line `hbone error: drain timeout`), or 20.0 s after
  SIGTERM in all (the ingress, the plain HTTP listener, with no such line and with the bind reporting Ok).

    the AgentgatewayParameters of each Gateway set `istio.enabled` (agw-central only) and `service.spec.type:

  In 2 of 32 removals -- NOT three, corrected after the B-5a review -- the line
  `error opentelemetry_sdk name="BatchSpanProcessor.Shutdown.Timeout""BatchSpanProcessor shutdown timing out."`
  stands between SIGTERM and the drain's start, and the drain then begins 5 001.0 ms / 4 999.99 ms after SIGTERM
  instead of 0.291-0.587 ms. `grep -rl 'BatchSpanProcessor.Shutdown.Timeout'` over all 32 oldpod.log returns exactly
  two files: dry/ingress-graceful/b5a-ingress-graceful-d1-01 and removal/ingress-rollout/b5a-ingress-rollout-r1-03.
  Among the THIRTY COUNTED repetitions there is exactly ONE. The third instance first claimed here was
  b5a-ingress-rollout-r1-05, whose only OTel lines are `BatchSpanProcessor.OnEnd.AfterShutdown` warnings written
  AFTER `binds drained` -- a different line, with no bearing on when the drain started; its drain began 0.414 ms
  after SIGTERM. Those AfterShutdown warnings appear in many repetitions and are visible in the quoted log above.
  The five seconds are this lab's own tracing configuration and not a property of the proxy: every emitter is at
  100 % sampling and the collector is in another namespace. The collector's own record for BOTH of those two pods is
  committed beside this file as collector-reading.txt, with its command and read stamp: each pod was still reaching
  the collector shortly before its own SIGTERM (1.400 s and 3.477 s before it) and neither reached it again
  afterwards, so the five seconds are the span processor's shutdown timing out rather than an unreachable collector.
  That reading covers those two pods only and is not generalised to the other thirty removals.
