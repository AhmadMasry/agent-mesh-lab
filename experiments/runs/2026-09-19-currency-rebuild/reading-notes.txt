                        orchestrator's installed distributions against uv.lock, the two ahead-of-chart images' warn lines,

                        mark its own close-after-read span: that span as it reads at THIS tree (not marked), by the committed
                        row that arms it; after-the-row.txt; clean-after/, the clean work item per receiver sent after it.
  retry-knobs/          readings.txt is the first take (18:33Z, then the last reading); readings-take-2-after-reading-h.txt
                        is the second (18:43Z), taken so that the knobs are the last reading again. Both end at 0 stanzas.

                        the state the cluster is left in; the first take is kept with a note above it.
  host-sleep.sh host-sleep.txt windows.csv host-sleep-assertion-counts.py
