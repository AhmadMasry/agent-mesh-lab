-- why one cleared-cache build was needed --
The first 'make orchestrator-image' after the project.toml change logged
  [builder]   Reusing cached layer /layers/paketo-buildpacks_python-package-managers-run/uv-env
and the image was unchanged. ShouldRun in python-package-managers-run v0.6.3
pkg/packagers/uv/uv_runner.go keys the layer on the uv.lock checksum, suffixed with a
hash of BP_UV_INSTALL_GROUPS when that is set, and on nothing else; no build variable
is part of the key. After 'pack build --clear-cache' the log read
  [builder]     Running 'uv sync'
and the layer was rebuilt without the dev group. The Makefile now carries
PACK_CLEAR_CACHE=1 for that case.
