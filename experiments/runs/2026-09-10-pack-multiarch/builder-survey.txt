Builder survey — read only, 2026-09-10
=======================================

A read of published image metadata, not a run: no image was pulled for use, no build was
made, and nothing on the cluster was touched. Recorded so the author can decide whether to
change builders; changing the builder is not part of this task.

Commands: `docker buildx imagetools inspect <ref>`, `docker manifest inspect --verbose
<ref>`, and the Docker Hub repository endpoint
`https://hub.docker.com/v2/repositories/paketobuildpacks/<repo>/`.

What this says
--------------
Under the pinned builder there is no digest that could make the orchestrator image follow
an arm64 host, because the builder publishes no arm64 image at any tag. The image is built
for amd64 and runs under emulation on this host. Two builders read here do publish arm64
indexes, `paketobuildpacks/builder-noble-java-tiny` and `heroku/builder:24`; neither was
tested against this project, and choosing one is the author's decision.
