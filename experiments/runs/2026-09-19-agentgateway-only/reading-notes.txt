1. strict/plaintext-probe.txt, first line: "(not enrolled: )" has an empty parenthesis.
   checks.sh meant to print the `istio.io/dataplane-mode` label of namespace `default` there, or the words "no
   dataplane-mode label". The jsonpath printed nothing at all, not even a newline, so the sed after it had no line
   to rewrite. The fact is in strict/posture.txt, read four seconds earlier: `default  <none>`. The prober was
   outside the mesh, which is also what its two refusals show.

2. strict/ztunnel-other-error-lines.txt is named for ztunnel lines OTHER than the probe's own, and holds 18 lines.
   One of the 18 IS the probe's: the refusal at the worker, stamped 11:41:36.070706Z, dst.workload the worker pod,
   "explicitly denied by: istio-system/istio_converted_static_strict". checks.sh excluded the probe's lines by
   `src.workload="mtls-probe"`, and that line carries `src.addr=` alone and no source workload. docs/walkthrough.md
   ("Plaintext is refused") warns of exactly this: the source fields are ztunnel's own attribution of a pod it has
   to have learned about, and on a prober created a moment earlier it may not have. The refusal at the
   orchestrator, 56 ms later, does carry src.workload="mtls-probe". So the file's other lines number 17:
     15  warn, direction="outbound", "no healthy upstream: 10.96.83.56:4318", 11:36:57.536Z to 11:37:10.727Z.
         10.96.83.56 is the otel-collector Service. Those 13 seconds are inside `make step-3` (11:36:30Z to
         11:38:21Z), after its overlay gave the agents their OTLP endpoint and before its collector chart was
         installed. No reading was in progress; the first one started at 11:38:43Z.
      2  "try_tcp_on_error: false", one per ztunnel pod: a line of the configuration ztunnel prints at start, kept
         by the pattern only because it holds the word "error".
   Lines of a policy rejection that are not the probe's: 0.

3. strict/legs.csv says `no-series` for two legs, for two different reasons, and neither is a leg that failed.
     "plaintext probe -> worker": the series exists, under another name. For the reason in note 2 its
       source_workload is `unknown`, and hops.py looked for `mtls-probe`. In strict/hops-security.csv it is the row
       `destination,unknown,unknown,worker.lab.svc.cluster.local,worker,lab,unknown,...,1`: one connection opened
       at the worker, connection security `unknown`, which is the refused plaintext connection. The probe's other
       row, to the orchestrator, is attributed and reads the same: `unknown`, 1.
     "agentgateway-ingress -> worker": no reading in this proof sent to the worker through the ingress. The clean
       check and the trace reach the worker in-cluster, through agw-central, and the ingress carried the
       orchestrator's SendMessage only (`agentgateway-ingress -> orchestrator`, mutual_tls, 1).
   The third `no-series` leg, "agw-central -> mockllm", is the one the table expects to be empty: neither end is
   ztunnel-captured (strict/posture.txt lists both as TCP), so ztunnel has nothing to report. It is this lab's
   plaintext external leg by design; that the calls arrived is the mock's invocation ledger, 1 per work item.
