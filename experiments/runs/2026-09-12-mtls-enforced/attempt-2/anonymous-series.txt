== The anonymous inbound series into worker, and what it is ==

Recomputing the istio_tcp_connections_opened_total deltas between readings A and B of
metrics.txt for destination_service=worker.lab.svc.cluster.local, reporter=destination,
leaves one series unaccounted for by name:

  source_workload="agentgateway-waypoint"  mutual_tls   A=8 B=9   (+1, the flows' own hop)
  source_workload="mtls-probe"             unknown      A=3 B=4   (+1)
  source_workload="unknown"                unknown          B=1   (+1, appears only in B)

The probe made TWO requests to the worker between the two readings -- the "before" phase's 200
and the "after" phase's refusal -- but the mtls-probe series gained only one. The other is the
anonymous series. It is not unexplained: ztunnel's own access log names it by omission.

Timeline, from this directory's files and from ztunnel's log (read-only; no cluster change):

  16:14:55Z         probe.sh phase=before starts; the prober pod was created moments earlier
                    (plaintext-probe-before.txt). The script requests worker first, orchestrator
                    second.
  16:14:56.634799Z  ztunnel: connection complete src.addr=10.244.1.163:42520
                    dst.addr=10.244.1.83:8080 dst.service="worker.lab.svc.cluster.local"
                    -- with NO src.workload field at all. 10.244.1.163 is the prober's pod IP.
  16:14:56.693603Z  ztunnel: the next connection, to the orchestrator, 59 ms later, DOES carry
                    src.workload="mtls-probe" (ztunnel-refusals.txt).
  16:14:56Z         reading A is taken, one second after the probe and inside the 15 s scrape
                    interval, so neither "before" connection had been scraped yet: both land in B.
  16:15:13Z         both "after" connections carry src.workload="mtls-probe"
                    (ztunnel-refusals.txt).

So the anonymous series is the prober's FIRST connection, made about a second after its pod became
ready and before ztunnel had resolved that pod's workload identity; the connection 59 ms later was
already resolved. It is the probe's own plaintext request counted without a name, not an
unidentified client: same source IP, same destination, same instant, and it carries the label
unknown for the same reason the access log line carries no src.workload.

It touches no hop of the two flows -- it is one of the two probe legs, which read unknown by
construction -- which is why hops.csv, whose rows are the named hops, does not carry it. Recorded
here so the A-to-B delta reconciles line by line.
